Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Wattle is made by an independent developer, Itay Tayeb. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs.

2. What we collect

  • Account: if you create an account, your email address and (optional) name; your password is stored only in hashed form. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and name they share with us. You can also use the app as a guest without an email.
  • Study data: your answers, test results, mistakes, saved questions, practice streak, values progress and, if you enter it, your planned test date. Used only to show your progress and personalise practice.
  • Error reports: if you use "Report an error" on a question, the reason and any text you write.
  • Notifications: if you turn on practice reminders, a push notification token for your device.
  • Purchases: whether you have full access and which plan. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Free-test limit: a random identifier created by the app and stored on your device, used only to allow one free practice test per device and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Stored only on your device: lesson reading progress, light/dark setting and a cached copy of your access status.

3. What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics tools, and we never sell your data.

4. Service providers who process data for us

Convex (app backend and database), RevenueCat (managing in-app purchases), Apple (App Store payments and Sign in with Apple), Google (only if you use Sign in with Google), Expo (delivering push notifications).

5. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and study data. The random free-test identifier is kept without any link to you, only to prevent repeated free tests on the same device. You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion of your data.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the citizenship test and is not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the bottom of this page.
